Owner, 2026-10-19, after eight full measurement runs were planned while he was home: *"that sort of run wouldn't have bothered me while I was at work, but yea when I'm on the PC it's kinda a pita lol"*.

His clarification, same day: *"im not saying dont do them, im saying i wont notice something like this when im not here because im well not here, im working normally"*. So this is NOT a restriction.

**How to apply:**
- **Run what the work needs, when it needs it.** Do not skip, shorten or delay a measurement because he is home.
- **When the timing is free** (a gate or a measurement that can wait), put the long ones while he says he is away or at work: he does not notice them then.
- **When he is on the PC,** announce before a desktop-taking run starts (announce-before-launching, in [[take-the-desktop-lock-dont-defer]]) and say roughly how many minutes it takes. Plan the cost in minutes before asking for many runs: map load dominates each run.
- His messages usually say when he is away ("i'm not home", "should be home in about 2 hours").
- Never trade a measurement's validity for convenience without saying so.
