Owner, 2026-10-19, after eight full measurement runs were planned while he was home: *"that sort of run wouldn't have bothered me while I was at work, but yea when I'm on the PC it's kinda a pita lol"*.

**How to apply:**
- **When he says he is away or at work:** that is the time for the long runs (a full gate, a many-run measurement, a TF2 session). Queue them then.
- **When he is on the PC:** keep desktop-taking runs short and few (two runs a side, no repeats for a sub-millisecond effect; plan the cost in minutes before asking for runs, map load dominates each run). Announce before one starts (announce-before-launching, in [[take-the-desktop-lock-dont-defer]]).
- **Ask or note when unsure** whether he is home; his messages usually say ("i'm not home", "should be home in about 2 hours").
- This is about his convenience, not correctness: never trade a measurement's validity for it without saying so.
