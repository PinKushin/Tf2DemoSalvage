beta.61 (1de685f1) and beta.62 (8eded5a0) published 2026-11-08, both Latest in turn. Supersedes [[handoff-2026-10-31]].

**B702 render-to-texture shadows, state:** casters (players, buildings, projectiles, items) into the 1024² atlas at
their shadow LOD (`StudioTriangles.ShadowLod`, sibling path `<model>#shadowlod`), alpha-tested and blended materials by
texture alpha; decals on world faces, displacements (node walk) and brush entities (`ShadowReceiverCull` = CullReceiver
+ 15-axis ComputeSeparatingPlane). Models take none — engine parity, studiorender's shadow pass draws no mesh. Owner of
every fact: `docs/findings/107-render-to-texture-shadows.md`.

**Open, in finding 107's list:** blob fallback past `r_shadowmaxrendered`; one decal list for every view (reflections,
monitors); `EF_NOSHADOW` / `EF_NORECEIVESHADOW` not decoded; corpses cast nothing; no live-TF2 comparison by looking
(no demo moment with a player over a brush entity found yet); surface bit `0x10` setter unknown (set from the brush
draw's NotDrawn texinfo set); every-frame cell redraw and re-projection (cost, not picture).

**How it went:** Fable reviews on each change (cavecrew-reviewer, model fable) caught a red (nodraw brush faces cut)
and two vacuous tests. Research agents (implementer, read-only, worktree) answered model receivers and caster
material/LOD from studiorender.dll. Doc pass by a sonnet bounded-task, fast-forwarded. See [[review-before-gate]].
