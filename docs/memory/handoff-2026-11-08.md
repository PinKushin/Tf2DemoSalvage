**Comparing against TF2 (learned the hard way this session):**
- `spec_goto` does nothing in a demo.
- `spec_mode 6` is POI. Hand-typed `spec_mode` turns the director off (B535).
- The viewer's `cl_interp` default is 0.1, where the owner's TF2 runs 0.0152.
- TF2's demoui HUD tick runs about 10 ticks ahead of its players while playing (B527).
- So: in-eye on a player (`spec_player X; spec_mode 4`), match interp and FOV (`demo_fov_override 90` on both), and shoot
  the viewer about 10 ticks before TF2's HUD tick. The f12 pair at viewer 6652 / TF2 HUD 6662 matches, scout shadow
  included (scratchpad `compare/`).
