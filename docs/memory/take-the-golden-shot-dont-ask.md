When a change is visual, capture TF2 itself at the same map, camera and setting (tf2 MCP: launch if no client answers, `map`/`setpos`/`setang`/ConVars, screenshot), and put the pair beside each other. Then ask the owner about the pair, not about our shot alone.

**Why:** owner, 2026-10-14, after being asked whether B537 bloom and B378 sun glow "look like TF2's" (he does not play with HDR, does not look at the sun): "you could have golden screenshotted either one at any time" and "tf2 has been free".

**How to apply:** any agent brief for a visible change includes the golden capture; never hand the owner an unpaired screenshot. Old demos must not play in modern TF2: load the map and place the camera instead. Related: [[take-your-own-screenshot]], [[compare-with-the-same-camera]], [[tf2-is-a-shared-fixture]].
