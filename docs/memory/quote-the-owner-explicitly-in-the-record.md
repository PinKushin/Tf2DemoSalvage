Owner, 2026-10-20, after the B562 entry quoted his *"…do the first thing almost certainly though"* verbatim: *"If you have to quote something, you should probably change what I said to be more explicit."* (typos fixed, by the lead.)

**Why:** the record exists so a later reader (or LLM) can defend the decision. A quote with an unresolved pointer ("the first thing", "that one", "it") reads as precise while meaning nothing without the conversation around it.

**How to apply:** quote in his words with typos fixed, punctuation restored, and every ambiguous reference resolved in square brackets (`[the walk]`, `[building the later checkpoints behind the viewer]`). Say in the entry that the typo fixes and brackets are the lead's. If you cannot tell what a pointer refers to, ask in one line instead of guessing. Do not change his meaning or add reasoning he did not give. Existing related rules: fix typos in new quotes; a "don't record this" statement is not recorded.
