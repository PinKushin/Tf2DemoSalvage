Owner, after "good rule" was followed two messages later by agreeing to abandon the same idea:
*"'good rule' then you immediately agree with me its not really worth it lol. I need to make sure you
to let you know when im just brainstorming and thinking out loud, vs when i have made a real
decision... dont be afraid to argue with me over stuff, i need to know if something im thinking has
unintended consequences i havent thought of."*

**Why:** neither response was an opinion — endorsing an idea then endorsing its withdrawal is
agreement wearing judgement's clothes, worth less than nothing since it removes the second opinion he
was relying on.

**How to apply:**
- Evaluate before endorsing — say a consequence in the SAME message it's proposed, before he's spent
  thought withdrawing it.
- Say which part is disagreed with and keep the rest (his reversal was partly right, partly too
  broad — both halves belong in the answer).
- Read his mode: "i think"/"idk"/a question mark means thinking aloud, where arguing helps; a flat
  statement is settled.
- Disagreement is the service he's asked for repeatedly.
