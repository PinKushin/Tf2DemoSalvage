## Rules learned this session
- **Never chain a push after a gate command without reading its result.** It happened once: `0979f203` was pushed
  before the gate's result was read, and the gate then showed B632 (RISKS B632, "Gate note").
- **A measurement record is committed before the run** (method, predictions, falsifiers), then the result is appended.
- **Every agent batch got an independent-model review**, and the Opus reviews found real bugs in 4 of the 5 decal batches
  (B635 batches 1-5: the exemption keyed on fog mode, the Modulate brush with no blended pass, a dead sort-group check that
  claimed parity, offset twins). Keep the review; do not merge on the implementer's green.
- **Parity fixes that cost frames are reported as such** (B624 is +0.042 ms a root rebuild, written as a cost in the notes).
- A verdict that gives a reason ("why it hid") is a claim; B635's was first interpolated, then measured different (B636).
