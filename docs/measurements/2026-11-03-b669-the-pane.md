# B669's remainder: what the far pane adds, the pipes behind it, and the wall behind them

## Observation

B669's record (`docs/RISKS.md`), measured by this project against live TF2 build 11191711: far camera `-3722 -1403 726 25 99`, patch x 800-1100 y 270-330, reads 228.1 in TF2 and 178.0 in ours. Fitting `with = (1-a)*without + X` over the gap tiles gave TF2 a 0.31, X 106 and ours a 0.25, X 43, though the texture's mean alpha is 65/255. TF2 shows a wire pattern and a blue cast at this distance, ours neither (`D:/measure-f12/b669/rem/z1.png`). With the glass hidden the pipes stay 12-28% short, and the wall and gap read brighter in ours (139.8 against 126.5, 41.1 against 24.4).

## Question

1. Of X, how much is the pane's reflection and how much its lightmapped diffuse, in each renderer?
2. Why does TF2's fitted a differ from the texture's alpha, and is there a second layer?
3. Why do the pattern and the cast differ?
4. Are the pipes' and wall's residues light (they move under `mat_fullbright`) or albedo?

## Method

Live TF2, one listen server on `cp_process_f12`, `spec_mode` 7, `spec_goto -3722 -1403 726 25 99`; `sv_cheats 1`, `mat_hdr_level 2`, `mat_force_tonemap_scale 1`, `mat_disable_bloom 1`; every cvar read before it is changed and read back after. One switch at a time, each shot compared in the patch only (B677):

- `r_drawtranslucentworld 0`: glass hidden.
- `mat_specular 0`: the reflection removed (SDK `lightmappedgeneric_dx9_helper.cpp:167`).
- `mat_fullbright 1`, then `mat_fullbright 2`: the lightmap, then the albedo, substituted.
- `r_drawbrushmodels 0`: whether a brush entity contributes a layer.
- `mat_forceaniso`, `mat_trilinear`, `mat_picmip`: read, for the pattern.

Ours: `tf2demoview f12.dem --tick 45091 --shot` at the same camera with `+mat_force_tonemap_scale 1 +mat_disable_bloom 1`, Debug, under `run-exclusive.ps1`, and the same switches where they reach a shot.

Numbers are mean linear luminance x1000 per box, by the boxes in `D:/measure-f12/b669/tf2set.py`.

## Control

TF2's own reference reproduced at 228.1 before any manipulation; the concrete box x 560-700 y 500-600, which reads 187.0 in both and must stay there under every switch but the fullbright ones. A switch that leaves the patch byte-identical did not reach the draw and answers nothing (B671).
