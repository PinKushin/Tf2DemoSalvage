## Verdict

**B624 costs about +0.04 ms per root rebuild (+9% of the roots total, 0.488 to 0.530)**, of which about 0.017 is layer sampling
(2.9 more samples), 0.007 compose, 0.004 the lock bracket and the rest in blend and ik (the added layers' weight work and IK
rules). This is the price of matching Valve's `AccumulatePose`, not a regression to chase; the run-to-run spread of one set
is about 0.02, so the move is real but its split is approximate. Against the start of today's fps work (roots 0.608 in the
setup-parts runs, before the frame-pair and dense-pose changes), the roots total is now 0.530: about 0.08 ms lower even with
Valve's autolayers in it, the frame-pair change and the dense pose having cut base + layer sampling by 0.06-0.09 ms.
