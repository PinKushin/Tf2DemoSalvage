# B669: which lights reach the pipes behind the pane in TF2 and not in ours

## Observation

`measurements/2026-11-03-b669-behind-the-pane.md`, by this project against live TF2 11191711: lighting only, placement #215 lit at (-3459 -683 506), facing -y, TF2's direct lights 0.201 against ours about 0.14; its bounce 0.115 against 0.1147. With the visibility trace off, ours overshoots to 0.415.

## Question

Which lights make up TF2's 0.201, and why does ours not take them: the trace's start, mask or rejection rule, a cvar's other path, or per-vertex data feeding this prop in TF2?

## Method

Read in disassembly: the cvar at `DAT_18075b318`, `FUN_1801b99a0`'s path, `FUN_1801b9570`'s rejection, the trace filter, and the static handle's light list. In TF2, `-vulkan`, `r_visualizelighttraces` and `r_worldlights`, cvars read first and read back after. In ours, the `prop-shade` list of every light's trace result and strength at the lighting origin, and the -y share of each, against the TF2 reading.

Numbers are grey-albedo light, `mat_fullbright 2` luminance over 0.2158, unless marked.

## Control

Lower pipe's -z face: 0.043 in TF2 against 0.039 in ours, which any subset must not break. The bounce's 0.115 stays. A switch that does not change the shot did not reach the draw.

## Environment

Machine as in the records above. TF2 build from `version`. Ours: branch head at the run.

## Result

Ours: `prop-shade` at the branch head, shares of each light computed from its strength and direction. TF2: build 11191711, `-vulkan`, `mat_hdr_level 2`.

**A correction to the question's own reading.** The earlier "+y 0.021" came from x 200-220 of the back shot, and a camera at yaw 270 puts -x on the right: that was the neighbouring placement. On #215's side (x 830-1200, rows 340-480) the back reads 0.03 x1000, zero. The tinted light found there, r/g 0.84, is the map's `emit_surface` colour, and ours predicts about the same for a point on that side (+y 0.0239 at (-3203 -683 506); *interpolated:* that placement's model and lighting origin were not read). Before the error was found, it drove a search for how a surface light could pass a detail brush. There was no such pass.

**Read in disassembly, `engine.dll`:**

- `FUN_1801b8350` stores the lump's lighting origin at `+0x184` unchanged and `FUN_1801ba590` passes that to `FUN_1801b8270`, so the trace starts there. `r_DrawModelLightOrigin` draws its cross at (640, 400) from `-3460 -800 560 20 90`, the projection of (-3459 -683 506).
- `FUN_1801b9570`: `TraceRay`, mask `0x4081`, the world-only filter unless flag 4, and rejected past 8 units short. `TraceRay` (`FUN_18018e870`) with that filter calls `FUN_180168cb0` on head node 0 and stops: static props do not block. Displacements do (`FUN_180167e40`).
- `FUN_180167e40` and `FUN_180167050` clip each leaf brush whose contents meet the mask. `FUN_18016bfc0` is a plain slab test on box brushes. None of them reads `CONTENTS_DETAIL`.
- `DAT_18075b318` chooses `FUN_1801b9570` directly or through `FUN_1801b99a0`, and both trace from the same point. The second only skips the radius test. **Which cvar it is stays unconfirmed:** `FUN_18000ff90` was not read. At #215 it cannot matter here: the only lights the trace passes and reach refuses are three of strength 0.
- `FUN_1801bb4b0`: `r_radiosity 0` zeroes the cube and leaves the light loop's skip flag at 0.
- **Baked data does not feed this prop.** The reading moved from `r_radiosity` and `r_worldlights`, which do not touch a colour mesh.

**No subset fits.** Reaching +z 0.063 takes two of the first three, about -y .021, and then all the rest together add -y .027 for a total near .048, short of .065. All of them give -y .070 and +z .118. The lights with the -y share and no +z (`(-3584 -1112 448)` and `(-3400 -1112 448)`, -y .0625 together) carry -z .0084 and are behind three and two world brushes.

Scanning the start point over x ±128, y ±64, z -24..+120 fits best at (-3331 -675 498), -y .198 +z .392 -z .004, and along y -643 z 514-538. Neither is a place the engine names: the origin itself gives -y .178 +z .428, and #214's point -y .174 +z .343 -z .013.

**Control:** `r_dynamic 0` left rows 349-379 to the digit (76.0, 72.7, 70.2, 66.5, with the states rebuilt to `r_radiosity 4`, `r_worldlights 4`), so no dlight supplies it. `mat_fullbright 2` row 359 read 43.4 with the lights alone, and the bounce's 0.115 stays.

**`r_visualizelighttraces` was not got.** Its value 2 draws only a rejected trace, red, for 3 s (`FUN_1800b9bd0`). A `prop_dynamic` spawned with it on drew nothing. Setting it, `host_timescale 0.02`, and rebuilding the static states by changing `r_worldlights` and `r_radiosity` ended the game: RCON closed and `tf_win64.exe` was gone, a crash. *Interpolated:* the overlay's capacity. `config.cfg` is unchanged, `mat_hdr_level "0"`.

## Verdict

**Not explained, and no fix was made.** The trace's start, mask, rejection rule and world-only filter match, and neither a static prop nor baked data is the source. No subset of the map's lights and no named start point gives TF2's two faces. A surplus equal on -y and +z with nothing on +y or -z is what remains, about 0.065 of 0.201 and 0.063 of 0.391. The neighbouring side may be short the same way: TF2 about 0.236 on -y and 0.514 on +z, ours 0.186 and 0.480 at the interpolated point above, so it does not yet count as a second sample.

What would settle it is the set of traces the engine rejected at this point, seen directly. The cvar crashed the game on a mass rebuild, so the next attempt is a breakpoint at `0x1801b9570`, which the game's `-insecure` launch would allow but which attaches to the owner's game: that is the owner's call.
